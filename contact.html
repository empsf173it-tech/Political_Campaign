Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about campaign activities and volunteering.

How can I request Marcus Vance for a speech?

Submit an inquiry using the Press & Media option above, specifying event date, venue, and estimated attendance.

Where do I pick up campaign yard signs?

Yard signs are available at any of our three regional offices during standard operating hours (9 AM - 6 PM).

Can high school students volunteer?

Yes. Student volunteers earn community service hours for door canvassing and digital phone banking.

How can I submit policy ideas?

You can write directly to our policy drafting committee via the contact form above.
