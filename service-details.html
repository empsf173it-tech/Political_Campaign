Economic Prosperity & Job Creation

A comprehensive roadmap for expanding local small business opportunities, attracting tech investments, and protecting worker pensions.

5-Point Legislative Plan

Building an Economy That Works for Everyone

Our district has immense potential, but outdated regulations and high commercial utility rates have held back small business growth. Marcus Vance’s economic policy introduces targeted legislative measures to revitalize our commercial corridors.

  • 1

    Small Business Capital Reserve

    Establishes a $50M state-backed low-interest loan pool for local retailers, restaurants, and service providers.

  • 2

    Green Technology Incubators

    Provides tax credits to clean tech firms that relocate research headquarters to our district.

  • 3

    Trade & Vocational Apprenticeships

    Partners local high schools with union apprenticeship programs to prepare students for high-paying skilled trade careers.

  • 4

    Commercial Energy Relief

    Caps peak electricity rate spikes for small storefronts operating under 20 employees.

  • 5

    Worker Pension Guarantee

    Protects retirement security for public employees and municipal workers.
